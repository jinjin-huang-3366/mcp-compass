# Launch channel playbook

Read this reference when preparing a multi-channel launch or adapting one release across several communities. Verify current platform limits and community rules at execution time.

## Shared campaign brief

Before drafting, capture:

- the release/tag or exact deployed commit;
- the developer problem and intended audience;
- two to four shipped, verifiable proof points;
- material limitations or separate components that are not deployed;
- one canonical call-to-action URL;
- the referral naming convention and success signal.

Keep one fact sheet but write each channel independently.

## X

Lead with one concrete developer problem or product outcome. Prefer a compact standalone post; use a thread only when the release needs evidence or a short walkthrough that cannot fit clearly. Keep links and media references explicit in the approval package.

## LinkedIn

Use a concise narrative: developer problem, what shipped, why the implementation is credible, and an invitation to try it or offer feedback. Avoid corporate filler and unsupported market claims.

## Bluesky

Use direct, conversational copy and a small number of relevant terms. Do not assume Typefully supports the account; verify the connected destination before creating a draft.

## Product Hunt

Prepare the product name, tagline, short description, maker comment, launch assets checklist, and first-comment plan. Treat preparation and submission as separate actions; submission requires explicit approval.

## Hacker News

Use a factual `Show HN:` title and explain what was built, who it helps, how it works, and what remains rough. Prefer technical substance over launch language. Never coordinate votes.

## Reddit

Select communities only after checking their current self-promotion and flair rules. Tailor the post to each community, disclose the maker relationship, and avoid duplicating the same copy across subreddits. Never automate votes or evade moderator restrictions.

## Measurement and feedback

MCP Compass uses Vercel Web Analytics for cookie-free page views and initial-page referrers. The current Hobby
dashboard reports referrer domains but does not expose UTM dimensions, so measure this launch by source domain and
preserve every publication URL. UTM-tagged links may still be prepared for portability, but do not claim those
dimensions are visible until the analytics plan supports them. Group feedback by theme, link to the original source,
and separate bugs, feature requests, questions, and positioning signals before proposing GitHub issues.
